## Current understanding

The participants currently understand XBRL GL Next to be an XBRL
International development project undertaken collaboratively by members and
participants from XBRL International, XBRL Europe and XBRL Japan.

The project uses the semantic content of XBRL Global Ledger 2017 as its
principal starting point, applies the FSM, BSM and LHM/HMD modelling
methodology defined in ISO 21926, and develops syntax bindings for the XBRL
Open Information Model, including xBRL-CSV.

This description is subject to confirmation by the appropriate XBRL
International authority. Participation by an individual does not by itself
constitute formal endorsement by XBRL International, XBRL Europe or XBRL
Japan.

## Effect of pending official confirmation

XII official confirmation remains pending. Until it is received:

- no artefact is described as an XII or XBRL International approved
  specification, official taxonomy, official work product, or official
  publication;
- no endorsement, maintenance commitment, or trademark permission by XII,
  XBRL International, XBRL Europe, or XBRL Japan is claimed;
- the pending XII IPR, contribution, repository-ownership, and official
  publication questions remain open; and
- a merge to the default branch, tag, GitHub Release, or repository visibility
  change requires separate approval.

Private repository access control is not a substitute for a confidentiality
agreement, contribution agreement, intellectual-property licence, or formal
work-product process.

## Separate project-owned experimental publication

Pending XII official confirmation and project-owner approval of an independent
experimental derivative are separate decisions. On 2026-08-27, the project
owner approved publication of a non-endorsed experimental derivative that is
not an XII, XBRL International, or XBRL Japan official work product,
specification, taxonomy, publication, or endorsement.

For this release only, that derivative uses the provisional namespace base:

`https://www.xbrl.or.jp/taxonomy/xbrl-gl-next/experimental/2026-12-31/`

The namespace is an experimental identifier. Its use does not imply XBRL
Japan approval, maintenance support, trademark permission, endorsement, or
selection as a future official namespace. If an official namespace or official
publication policy is established, migration will occur in a separate release.
